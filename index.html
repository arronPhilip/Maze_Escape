Maze Escape

Reach the exit before the guard reaches you. A new maze is generated every game, and the guard always knows the shortest way to you.

Controls: arrow keys or WASD, or swipe on the board. How the guard works: every turn it runs a breadth-first search from its own cell to yours, then steps along that shortest path. Turn on "Guard's route" to see it. The maze is built with randomised depth-first search, then extra walls are removed so there is always more than one way around.
